Who I am

About

I'm Kobe Christian Balatbat, an operations professional with experience supporting real estate and Australian mortgage teams. I work comfortably between the details of daily execution and the systems that keep it organized.

My approach starts with understanding the process: where information enters, who owns the next step, and where delays or errors usually happen. From there, I clean the data, clarify the handoffs, and add automation only where it genuinely helps.

A BS in Computer Engineering gives me the technical foundation to build with tools such as Zapier, n8n, and Google Apps Script, while my operations background keeps the result practical for the people using it every day.

Workflows Managed & Improved
